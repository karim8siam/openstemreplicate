FOR AUTHORS & STUDENT RESEARCHERS

Submit research for review.

Suggest a published STEM paper, preprint or completed scientific project. Our team decides whether it fits the monthly editorial selection; submission does not guarantee acceptance.

Research submission

This form is configured to send submissions to shahriyarkarimsiam@gmail.com through FormSubmit. The mailbox owner must activate the form using the first confirmation email before deliveries are assured.

Upload only materials you have permission to share. Prefer DOI or authorized repository links for copyrighted papers. Please do not upload private patient data, unpublished confidential research, or personal records.

FormSubmit may display a human verification screen. Initial activation is mandatory. Alternatively email us directly.

OUR EDITORIAL PROCESS
  1. 01
    Receive

    Your suggestion arrives in the review inbox after the form is activated.

  2. 02
    Verify

    We examine STEM relevance, scholarly identity, DOI/source, and publication status.

  3. 03
    Select

    We may request clarification. Only accepted research enters a future edition.

  4. 04
    Publish

    We write our own summary, credit the publication and link primary sources.

Nothing is published automatically.

This form sends a proposal to an editorial inbox; there is no public upload area, unreviewed database entry or automatic Google indexing.
