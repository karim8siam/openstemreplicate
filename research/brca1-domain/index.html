Patient-derived separation-of-function mutations reveal specific role for BRCA1 coiled coil domain in genome stability

Bernadett Szikriszt, Eszter Németh, Bayejid Hosen et al.

Published 2026-09-22Nature CommunicationsDOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-77330-5 ↗
Read original publication ↗
OPENSTEM EDITORIAL SUMMARY

What the research examines

This molecular-biology study investigates how specific patient-derived BRCA1 mutations affect genome stability and distinct protein functions. It adds mechanistic evidence to research on DNA repair.

Why it may matter

Genome maintenance, disease-associated variation and molecular mechanisms.

What to examine carefully

Molecular findings should not be used as individual medical advice.

Editorial scope: This is a concise, independently written introduction based on the publisher information, not a full systematic review or independent replication. It must not replace reading the original methods, limitations and conclusions.
