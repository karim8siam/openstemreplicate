Programming semiconducting two-dimensional conjugated metal–organic frameworks via anisotropic reticular chemistry

Published 2026-09-24Nature ChemistryDOI: 10.1038/s41557-026-02251-1 ↗

OPENSTEM EDITORIAL SUMMARY

What the research examines

Researchers investigate the synthesis and properties of semiconducting two-dimensional metal–organic frameworks using directional chemical design. The work contributes to tunable electronic materials.

Why it may matter

Reticular chemistry and potential electronic materials.

What to examine carefully

Device applications need independent evaluation beyond initial material characterization.
