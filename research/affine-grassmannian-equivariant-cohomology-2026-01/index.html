Equivariant cohomology and intersection theory of affine Grassmannians for reductive groups

Maximilian Vogel, Sanjay Bannerjee, Ling-Jun Wang et al.

Published 2026-01-13Journal of the American Mathematical SocietyDOI: 10.1090/jams/1032 ↗
Read original publication ↗
OPENSTEM EDITORIAL SUMMARY

What the research examines

Establishes an explicit combinatorial formula for the torus-equivariant intersection cohomology of Schubert varieties in the affine Grassmannian, connecting geometric Satake equivalence with quantum Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov differential equations.

Why it may matter

Unifies modern geometric representation theory, quantum integrable systems, and algebraic topology.

What to examine carefully

Restricted to complex reductive algebraic groups and does not cover loop groups over fields of positive characteristic.

Editorial scope: This is a concise, independently written introduction based on the publisher information, not a full systematic review or independent replication. It must not replace reading the original methods, limitations and conclusions.
