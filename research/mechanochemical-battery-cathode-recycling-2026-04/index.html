Solid-state recycling of end-of-life lithium-ion battery cathodes via low-temperature mechanochemical re-lithiation

Guo-Hua Ding, Samuel Baker, Priya Ramanathan et al.

Published 2026-04-28ACS Energy LettersDOI: 10.1021/acsenergylett.6b00892 ↗
Read original publication ↗
OPENSTEM EDITORIAL SUMMARY

What the research examines

A sustainable closed-loop direct recycling process uses ball-milling with lithium acetate and mild thermal annealing (400 °C) to restore degraded NMC-811 cathode powders back to pristine crystal stoichiometry, avoiding harsh acid leaching and chemical wastes.

Why it may matter

Offers a circular economic solution for the impending deluge of spent electric vehicle battery packs.

What to examine carefully

Severely degraded cathodes with excessive aluminum current-collector contamination require preliminary sorting and solvent stripping.
