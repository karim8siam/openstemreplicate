Aharonov–Bohm interference in a Z₂ lattice gauge theory on a hybrid qubit–oscillator quantum computer

S. Saner, O. Băzăvan, D. J. Webb et al.

Published 2026-09-25Nature PhysicsDOI: 10.1038/s41567-026-03400-6 ↗
Read original publication ↗
OPENSTEM EDITORIAL SUMMARY

What the research examines

This work uses a hybrid qubit–oscillator architecture to investigate interference associated with a lattice gauge theory. It provides an example of controlled analogies between quantum information hardware and theoretical physics.

Why it may matter

Simulation methods for gauge-field phenomena.

What to examine carefully

Study-specific assumptions and error mitigation should be checked in the original article.

Editorial scope: This is a concise, independently written introduction based on the publisher information, not a full systematic review or independent replication. It must not replace reading the original methods, limitations and conclusions.
