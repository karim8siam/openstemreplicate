OPENSTEM POLICIES

Privacy.

These disclosures describe how the current editorial website works. Updated: 20 October 2026.

Submitted information

Research submissions are sent to a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit) for delivery to the listed Gmail address. Do not send restricted, medical or unpublished confidential materials. The provider processes submitted form data and attachments subject to its own policies.

Local storage

Saved paper bookmarks are stored only in this browser via localStorage and are not synchronized to the server. You can clear browser site storage to remove them.

External scholarly sources

Original paper links navigate to publishers and DOI resolvers, which have independent privacy policies. Discord links open the external Discord platform.

Contact and requests

For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning information sent to our team, contact shahriyarkarimsiam@gmail.com. We do not promise immediate deletion from an independent third-party provider.
