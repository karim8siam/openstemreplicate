# Editorial policy — 20 STEM highlights per calendar month

## What this is

An **editorial shortlist** of 20 notable and useful STEM research papers published in a selected calendar month, with independently written accessible notes and authentic DOI/publisher citations. It is NOT a formal world ranking, peer-review badge, bibliometric league table, or statement of independent scientific reproduction.

## Eligibility

- Substantial STEM focus: natural sciences, technology, engineering, and mathematics (including computational interdisciplinary research).
- Traceable original published research or a clearly labeled preprint with trustworthy primary source and actual date.
- Source identity checked (DOI or recognized repository), correct title, authors, date, article type, and external publisher link.
- No fake journals, automatically manufactured authors, private contacts, scraped full-text dumps, or unsupported claims.
- Research submissions from students are welcome but editorially screened against these criteria.

## Editorial scoring (internal, not published as authoritative rankings)

Review each candidate in the context of: educational relevance (0–5), methodological/reproducibility interest (0–5), significance to field (0–5, evidence-backed), reliability of source metadata (0–5), cross-STEM portfolio diversity (qualitative) and clarity for a student audience. Use human review and discuss uncertainties. Do not present these scores as objective impact assessments. Preserve an internal review log with reviewer, date and publisher check.

## Scientific wording

Use careful language: 'the authors report', 'the study investigates', 'may support'. Distinguish experimental evidence from simulations, correlations from causal claims, and preprints from peer-reviewed publications. Avoid biomedical advice.

## Correcting the record

Allow users to report mistakes via shahriyarkarimsiam@gmail.com. Inspect verified sources, edit the monthly JSON, rerun tests, rebuild and redeploy. If a paper is retracted, display the official status prominently after confirmation.

## Contributor details

Show only source-backed public institutional affiliations and publisher-listed corresponding author emails. Never imply a single country represents all authors of an international collaboration. For most sample entries, affiliations are intentionally left to the linked article rather than guessed.

### September 2026 pilot caveat
All 20 initially verified research highlights link to Nature Portfolio / Springer Nature publications. This is a practical first pilot batch, **not** a representative or comprehensive global shortlist. Diversify research publishers and sources in later monthly editions.
