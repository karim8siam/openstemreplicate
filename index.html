Causal evidence that language models use confidence to drive behaviour

This study investigates how confidence-related signals affect language-model decisions about when to answer or abstain. It provides experimental evidence that changes to such signals can influence model behaviour.

Nature Machine Intelligence·Dharshan Kumaran et al.

A collaborative agent with two lightweight synergistic models for autonomous crystal materials research

The study presents an AI-agent arrangement using two smaller cooperating models for materials-research tasks. It examines a way to combine reasoning and scientific tool use without relying only on very large models.

Nature Machine Intelligence·Tongyu Shi et al.

OUR PUBLISHING POLICY

Deliberately selective, openly sourced.

We only publish the research we can substantiate. Each monthly edition is editorially reviewed before release.

How we select ↗
01

Nine reviewed editions

We curate 20 peer-reviewed papers for each month from January 2026 through September 2026, publishing in reverse-chronological order.

02

Trace the source

Every featured paper includes a persistent DOI, author names, publication venue, and a route to the original record.

03

Submit and contribute

Researchers can suggest their work for editorial review. Publication here is never automatic or guaranteed.
