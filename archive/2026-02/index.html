Atomic-resolution structure of eukaryotic ribosome-associated chaperone complex during nascent chain folding

Single-particle cryo-EM resolves the human ribosome-associated complex (RAC) and Hsp70 chaperone Ssb positioned directly over the ribosomal exit tunnel at 2.1 Å resolution, showing how hydrophobic nascent polypeptide segments are captured before misfolding.

Using the Square Kilometre Array (SKA) pathfinder arrays, astronomers detect circularly polarized, low-frequency (35-50 MHz) coherent radio bursts modulated by the orbital period of an Earth-mass rocky exoplanet, indicating an active planetary magnetosphere.

Ultrafast optical switching of magnetic skyrmions in chiral antiferromagnetic thin films

Single femtosecond circular laser pulses deterministically write and erase sub-20-nanometer magnetic skyrmions in synthetic antiferromagnetic multilayers within 1.2 picoseconds, with zero skyrmion Hall effect deflection.
