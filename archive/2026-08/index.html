Observation of coherent magnon-polariton states in van der Waals antiferromagnets

Researchers report strong coherent coupling between optical photons and collective spin excitations in 2D van der Waals antiferromagnetic flakes inside a microcavity. The observed anti-crossing demonstrates hybrid magnon-polariton formation at low temperatures.
