Metagenomic profiling of uncultured viral communities in the deep subseafloor sedimentary biosphere

Through deep metagenomic sequencing of International Ocean Discovery Program (IODP) sediment cores up to 1,200 meters beneath the seafloor, microbiologists uncover thousands of previously unknown bacteriophages actively regulating microbial mortality in extreme nutrient-starved environments.

Physicists accelerate a rubidium Bose-Einstein condensate to supersonic flow speeds using stepped optical potentials, forming an acoustic sonic horizon. Spatial density-density correlation measurements confirm spontaneous emission of thermal phononic analog Hawking radiation.
